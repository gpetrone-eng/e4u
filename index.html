English Future Tenses Video Guide (Will vs Going To vs Present Continuous)

1. Future Simple (WILL)

Spontaneous Decisions & Opinions

  • Instant Decisions: "I'll open the door!"
  • Personal Predictions: "I think it will rain."
  • Promises & Offers: "I will help you."

2. BE GOING TO

Intentions & Physical Evidence

  • Prior Plans: "I'm going to study engineering next year."
  • Present Evidence: "Look at those black clouds! It's going to rain."

3. Present Continuous

Fixed Personal Arrangements

  • Arranged Appointments: "I am seeing the doctor tomorrow at 10 AM."
  • Tickets & Dates: "We are flying to Paris on Friday."
